# The Emetos Tutor

Welcome. This is a practice copy made just for you: change it, break it, nothing here matters. Open the tutor
again (Help > Emetos Tutor) and you get a fresh copy.

Each lesson explains a little and then gives you exercise lines. A line marked `--->` is the one to change;
make it look like the line under it, marked `ok>`.

## Lesson 1: Moving around

Move the cursor with the arrow keys. {{key:cursorWordEndRight}} jumps a word to the right and
{{key:cursorWordLeft}} a word to the left. {{key:cursorTop}} goes to the top of the file and {{key:cursorBottom}}
to the bottom. {{key:workbench.action.gotoLine}} asks for a line number and goes there.

Go to the end of this file, then come back here.

---> The cat sat on the mat.mat
ok>  The cat sat on the mat.

Delete the extra word at the end of the line marked `--->`.
