# Glutter issues

Things Glutter is missing or gets wrong, found while building Emetos. Each entry says what is wrong, where Emetos works around it (if it does), and what the fix in Glutter would be. An entry is removed when the fix is in the Glutter version Emetos uses.

## WindowDecoration: a Leading widget cannot take the room that is left (9b.2, still so in Glutter 1.8.1)

- **What:** since Glutter 1.8 (28.18) only the title, the empty space around it and the 12-pixel gap before `Leading` move the window, maximize it and show its menu; `Leading` and `Actions` widgets get their own gestures. That is what Emetos's workspace tabs need (a drag, a double click and a right click on a tab are the tab's), so the menu and the tabs are `Leading` and the title is the empty bar. But `Leading` widgets are children of the bar's row: a widget with an unbounded width gets no limit, and a `Flexible` one shares the row with the title (`Expanded`, flex 1), so the tabs would get half of the bar at most, and the title would be left over by a loose one as dead space that does not move the window.
- **Where the workaround is:** `titleBarState.Build` (`internal/gui/workbench/titlebar.go`, the `// Glutter:` comment): the leading widgets are one row (the menu, the gap, the tabs as a loose `Flexible`) in a `ConstrainedBox` whose maximum width is the width of the window less what `WindowDecoration` keeps (its 12-pixel gap and 46 pixels for each button of the window, from the window's capabilities). The two numbers are Glutter's private constants (`windowButtonWidth`, the gap in `newWindowTitleBar`); if they change, the tabs end a few pixels too early or too late.
- **What Glutter could provide:** lay `Leading` and `Actions` out before the title and give the title what is left, with `Leading` as a loose flexible (the tabs take what they need, the title the rest), or tell the app the room it has: for example `WindowDecorationOpts.Leading` laid out with the bar's maximum width less the window buttons and the `Actions`, as the title is.

## Text with `TextOverflowEllipsis`: a line with no place to break gets only the ellipsis (12.7)

- **What:** a one line `RichText` with `Overflow: TextOverflowEllipsis` and text that is wider than the box and has no place to break (a call such as `fmt.Println(Shout(Greet("ev..."))` in a row of the peek view) shows nothing but the ellipsis, where Flutter keeps the characters that fit and ends them with the ellipsis. A text with spaces shows the words that fit and the ellipsis. With soft wrap on, a break after the ellipsis character `…` leaves only that on the one line. Seen in the first proof image of step 12.7; not reproduced with a test of its own.
- **Where the workaround is:** `peekState.refRow` and `peekOverflow` (`internal/gui/editor/peek_widget.go`, the `// Glutter:` comment): the rows of the results have no soft wrap, are ellipsized when they have spaces and clipped otherwise, and the text before the match is cut to its last characters, so the match stays on the row.
- **What Glutter could provide:** cut an unbreakable line at a character (a grapheme boundary) when it is the only way to make the ellipsis fit.
